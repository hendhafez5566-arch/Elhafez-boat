# Business rules

Published offers and programs are the only sources of customer-facing facts. Draft/disabled/expired items never appear in new selections. Availability is checked at request time and again before confirmation.
Provisional request is not confirmed booking. Capture price, currency, date, terms, occupancy and offer version snapshot. Confirmation requires authorized user and verified capacity/payment rules. Never silently change accepted terms.
No AI autonomous price overrides, refunds or booking confirmations.
Human handoff freezes bot automation for the conversation until explicit release. Follow-ups stop on opt-out, booking completion or other configured stop conditions.
Customer consent, purpose limitation and secure handling of traveler documents are mandatory. Staff roles restrict viewing and changing prices, bookings and customer data.
