# Product vision

Elhafez Boat is a subscription SaaS for multiple independent tourism companies. The company's staff manage offers and travel programs without programming. Their customers primarily navigate WhatsApp with interactive lists and reply buttons; WhatsApp Flows collect structured booking information. Free-text AI answers only using approved company data and routes back to menus or a human.

Product pillars: simple Arabic RTL dashboard; official WhatsApp integration; data-driven menu templates; sales and lead management; provisional booking; follow-ups with messaging compliance; company isolation; clear ownership and auditable changes.

User roles: platform owner, tenant owner, manager, sales supervisor, sales agent, reporting viewer.

Initial product scope: Umrah, Hajj, domestic and international tourism; offers, customers, conversations, bookings, reports, employee permissions, company configuration. Integration with legacy ERP is optional future work, never a dependency.
